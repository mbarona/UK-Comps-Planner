Everyone who connects to the same address sees the same plan, and edits merge. It stores one small file in your Google Drive.
How to set it up (about 5 minutes, once)
  1. Go to script.google.com and click New project.
  2. Delete the sample code, paste the script below, and click the save icon.
  3. Click Deploy, then New deployment. Click the gear and choose Web app.
  4. Set Execute as to Me and Who has access to Anyone. Click Deploy.
  5. Click Authorize access and choose your Google account. If Google warns the app is not verified, click Advanced, then Go to project, then Allow.
  6. Copy the Web app URL (it ends in /exec), paste it in the box above, and click a Connect button.
  7. Send the same URL to your team. Each person pastes it once on their own computer.
